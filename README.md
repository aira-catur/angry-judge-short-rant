Entry for the [Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046) competition on make.fun: a program that writes one short, funny rant (393 characters) about an everyday annoyance — the self-checkout machine that accuses you of stealing your own onions, freezes, and summons a human to clear you of smuggling a 40p banana, after which you realise you queued twice and did the machine's job unpaid.

## The rant

> The self-checkout was supposed to spare me small talk. Instead I now argue daily with a machine that thinks I'm stealing my own onions. "Unexpected item in the bagging area!" Yes, Brenda, they're called groceries — you scanned them. Then she locks up and summons a human to confirm I'm not a criminal mastermind smuggling a 40p banana. I queued twice, paid full price, and did her job. Unpaid.

## Why this one

Picked from 16 candidates over two harsh judging rounds scored on the judge's own criteria, in order: a funny rant about one specific everyday annoyance (not an essay or a list), actually funny rather than trying hard, concrete detail (onions, the bagging-area announcement, a 40p banana), economical wording with no throat-clearing, and a fresh angle — the shopper ends up an unpaid employee of the machine that accused them. A printer-out-of-cyan rant was the runner-up and lost on freshness: its premise is a meme the judge has heard a hundred times.

## Layout

- `main.ts` — entrypoint run by the grader (`node main.ts`, Node 22); writes the rant to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no randomness, no clock: byte-identical on every run.
